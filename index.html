SALAD DEFENDER NINJA

Mellow vs. the giant utensils

HOW TO PLAY
Mouse: aim
W: walk toward the mouse · S: back
A / D: sidestep
Click: throw the knife on a chain
It grabs fruit and toppings too
E: use the topping you're holding
Q: squirt the ketchup packet
Esc: menu
IN VR
Left stick: walk · Right stick: turn
Trigger: throw the knife
A: use topping · B: ketchup
THE PLAN
1. Grab every banana, mango and orange
2. They become a GIANT salad
3. Defend it from 17 utensils
Spoons pop up from underground
Forks spin and chase YOU
Knives go straight for the salad
